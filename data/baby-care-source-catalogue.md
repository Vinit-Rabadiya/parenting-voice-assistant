# Baby-care source catalogue

Collection date: 23 October 2026.

Scope: Kenya-focused, English-language caregiver support from birth through the
second birthday. This catalogue contains 71 source entries, combining existing
project references with newly located material. Entries are not 71 newly indexed
sources. Collection does not establish clinical approval or complete topic coverage.

## Collection status

- Original PDFs retained locally: existing development cards, Reach Up manual,
  Kenya handbook, Kenya community guidelines and Birth to Five 2025; newly
  downloaded Birth to Five 2026 and the Jeong research paper.
- Website articles: original URLs retained, not website HTML. Links opened during
  this collection are listed below; previously prepared source entries are labelled.
- WHO book/guideline entries: official publication pages checked. Full-document
  review and page selections remain pending unless already recorded elsewhere.
  The complementary-feeding PDF download returned a non-PDF response and was not
  saved. A reachable publication page is not a successful PDF download.
- Research: distinguish a downloaded paper from an abstract or a full-text review.
  These papers support the research/design rationale, not automatic symptom advice.
- Selected candidate passages have been uploaded to the separate
  `caregiver_guidance_practical_review_v1` collection for development evaluation.
  They have not been activated in the application. Existing frozen revisions are unchanged.

For each selected passage, retain its source, exact section/page, age conditions,
possible explanations, practical steps, precautions, warning signs and review
decision. Do not flatten a book into unrestricted advice for every age.

## Remaining collection and selection gaps

- Obtain a complete current official Kenya routine immunisation schedule and resolve
  later updates and regional eligibility. C59 alone does not satisfy this.
- Review Kenya-applicable water, formula preparation, storage and transport guidance
  as a complete workflow. Do not blend incompatible instructions across countries.
- Expand exact sections for weather/clothing, safe carrying, outings, siblings and
  handover between caregivers; broad handbooks are starting points, not proof of
  sufficient extracted depth.
- Review premature infants and additional needs separately; chronological-age
  passages cannot automatically answer individualized developmental concerns.
- Check original review dates, revisions, copyright and reuse terms before extracting
  substantial material. Public access does not imply permission to republish a book.
- Review conflicts and duplicated advice, then prepare traceable passages and
  meaningful alternatives. Do not automatically index clinical manuals, whole books,
  citations, abstracts or this catalogue.

Related issues: #76 source coverage; #100 vaccination; #102 common concerns;
#103 response handling; #74/#44 retrieval and evidence checks; #78/#79 answer
quality and qualified review. Source collection alone does not close these issues.
